## Project facts to keep consistent

- The recommendation covers eight methods (`METHOD_KEYS` in `causal_lab/causal_engine/recommendation.py`). Do not write another number without checking that list. ITS has no estimator in the app; DML has one but is not recommended.
- The question analyser (`causal_engine/question_reading.py`, `question_analysis.py`) gives an initial assessment. Never describe it, or the app, as establishing causal identification.
- No numerical credibility on methods: the order is argued. Do not add scores back. Identification Strength is gone from the app too (`docs/IDENTIFICATION_STRENGTH.md`): the function remains only for the calibration spikes.
- The 10% bias and 90% coverage rule evaluates the DiD simulation study only. It does not control the app or apply to other methods.
- The DiD and the event study share one convention: clustered by unit, unit dummies not counted, Student t with G - 1 degrees of freedom. Any new clustered fit must follow it.
- **Scope:** decided in writing on 2026-11-09 (`docs/SCOPE_CLARIFICATION.md`): **the DiD bias study is the main contribution**; the question analysis is an extension, always presented with its numbers: precision 0.95 and recall 0.60 on the second set (the ones the professor cited), version 3's 1.00 and 0.83 on the third set, where version 2 scores 0.80, and version 3.1's 0.96 and 0.86 on the fourth set, where version 2 scores 0.79. Never give 0.83 or 0.86 alone. Never call the question analysis the principal contribution. Heterogeneity across units, the December assessment and issue #2 are still unanswered.
- **Evaluation:** `docs/evaluation/question_set.json`, `docs/evaluation/v2/question_set.json`, `docs/evaluation/v3/question_set.json` and `docs/evaluation/v4/question_set.json` are frozen, and `spike/evaluate_question_analysis.py` refuses to overwrite a result. Never add their questions to patterns or tests, and never change the analyser to score better on them. A corrected analyser needs a new set, with a declared protocol.
- **Do not attribute a threshold to an author** without reading the source. Do not report a kappa from an incomplete sheet (`spike/compute_kappa.py` refuses one).
- **Tests:** from `causal_lab/`, `../.venv/Scripts/python -m pytest` (about 6 minutes). CI also runs `openspec validate --specs`. Do not merge with a failing test, and do not merge without the user's approval.
- **Page, video, slides (Session 8):** after each step, commit and add a row to `PROCESS.md`: the step, what the tool did, and what the author decided (never write a decision the author did not make; say "agent's choice, to be confirmed"). The page is `docs/index.html`, the slides `docs/slides/index.html` (reveal.js, one file, no speaker notes), published by GitHub Pages from `/docs` on `main`. Every screenshot from a real run; every number with its source. Dates: page 14 November 20:00, video 21 November, slides 28 November, demos 1 and 8 December.
- **Deployment:** the online app is rebooted by the user at share.streamlit.io. Do not claim it is live until that has been verified.
